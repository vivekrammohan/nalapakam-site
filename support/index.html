Nalapakam support

Nalapakam is a personal recipe book for iPhone and iPad. It needs no account, and your recipes stay on your devices and in your own iCloud.

Get help

To ask a question, report a problem or suggest something, open an issue (a free GitHub account is needed). Say which iPhone or iPad you use and what you tried; leave out API keys and anything private.

Common questions

Do I need an API key? No. Recipes are read on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence where available, or with simple rules. A key for Claude, Gemini or GPT is optional and billed to your own account with that company. See how to get one.

Where are my recipes? On your iPhone, and in your iCloud Drive when iCloud is on. Settings › Backup keeps snapshots you can restore from.

Why does a YouTube video say it has no recipe? Nalapakam reads the recipe the cook wrote in the description or on a linked page. When there is none, add a Gemini key to read the video itself, or add the video file.
