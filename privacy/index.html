Nalapakam privacy policy

Updated 8 November 2026.

What the developer collects

Nothing. Nalapakam has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer never receives your recipes, photos, keys or anything else you put in the app.

What stays on your devices

Your recipes, their photos, your shopping list and your settings are kept on this iPhone, or in your own iCloud Drive when iCloud is turned on. API keys you add are kept in this iPhone’s Keychain and are never synced or shared.

Apple Intelligence, when your iPhone has it, reads recipes on this device. Text in photos is recognised on this device. Apple’s speech recognition turns speech in a video you add into text; Apple may process that audio on its servers when the language is not available on your iPhone, under Apple’s privacy policy.

AI services you connect with your own key

If you add an API key for Anthropic (Claude), Google (Gemini) or OpenAI (GPT), Nalapakam sends that company what is needed to read a recipe: the text of a note, a page or a video’s description you import, photos you import, frames and audio from a video you add (or, with a Gemini key, the video file itself, which Nalapakam deletes from Google once it is read and Google otherwise deletes after 48 hours), the link to a YouTube video you choose, and a recipe’s ingredient list for a nutrition estimate.

This goes straight from your iPhone to that company under your own account, and is billed to you. It is used only while the “Use my API keys” switch is on, or when you tap a button that names the service, which asks first while the switch is off. Each company handles it under its own terms: anthropic.com/legal/privacy, policies.google.com/privacy and openai.com/policies/privacy-policy.

Other services you choose

When you import a web page or a YouTube video, Nalapakam fetches that page, or the video’s title, description and thumbnail through YouTube’s official services. Cook mode plays YouTube videos in YouTube’s own player. Nalapakam uses YouTube API Services: by importing or playing a YouTube video you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service (youtube.com/t/terms), and Google’s Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy) applies to what YouTube receives. You can revoke Nalapakam’s access to YouTube data at any time through Google’s security settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions); Nalapakam does not sign in to your Google account; a recipe you save keeps the video’s title, channel, link and thumbnail, on your iPhone and in your iCloud, and nothing else from YouTube.

When you send a recipe or photo to the ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini app, it goes to that app, under its own terms.

A public link you create puts a copy of that recipe in your iCloud, readable by anyone with the link until it expires or you remove it in Settings › Shared links. Exporting to Reminders adds items to the Reminders list you pick.

Your control

You can remove a key, switch the keys off, turn Apple Intelligence off in Nalapakam, delete any recipe, or delete the app to remove everything stored on this iPhone. Copies in your iCloud Drive stay until you delete them there.

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support page.
